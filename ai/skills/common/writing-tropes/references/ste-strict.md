# Strict mode

Strict mode is the full structure of ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English). It applies to the step-by-step procedures that `SKILL.md` lists for strict mode. Every 80% Structure rule in `SKILL.md` applies, and these rules tighten them. Text on the exclusion list in `SKILL.md`, such as in-app help, stays out of strict mode too.

1. Number the steps, with one instruction in each step.
2. Start each step with an imperative verb ("Run", "Open", "Confirm").
3. Keep each procedure sentence to 20 words or fewer. Descriptive sentences keep the 25-word ceiling.
4. Put each warning or caution on its own line, before the step it applies to. Say what can go wrong and how to avoid it.
5. Use one verb for one action through the whole procedure. A step that says "run" keeps "run" in every later step.
6. After a step whose result the reader must check before going on, state the expected result.
7. Take vocabulary from the project's glossary when it has one. Otherwise pick one term for each concept and keep it. The ASD-STE100 dictionary is not available, so strict mode controls structure and term consistency, not an approved word list.
